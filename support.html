Common questions

How do I play? Tap a jar, then tap another jar to pour the top fireflies. You can only pour onto the same color or into an empty jar. Fill every jar with one color to win.

I bought "Remove ads" on another device. Open Settings (the gear on the main menu) and tap "Restore purchases".

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your iPhone and in iCloud when iCloud is on.
